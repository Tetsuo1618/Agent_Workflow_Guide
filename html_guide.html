HTML Document Structure

Every HTML page is like a house. It needs a solid foundation (DOCTYPE), walls and roof (<html>), a hidden utility room (<head>) with instructions for the browser, and a living room (<body>) where all your furniture (content) goes. Let's explore each room!

Basic Content Elements

Now that we have our house built, let's fill it with content! Headings create a clear outline, paragraphs hold your text, links connect to other pages, images add visuals, and a few special tags help with spacing. Think of these as your basic decorating tools.

Organising Content

Sometimes you need to organize information neatly — like a grocery list, a dictionary, or a spreadsheet. HTML gives you three powerful tools: lists for grouped items, description lists for terms and definitions, and tables for rows and columns of data.
